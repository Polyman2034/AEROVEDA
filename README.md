# AEROVEDA

> **AI-powered environmental intelligence for detecting, understanding, and predicting hyper-local air pollution events.**

**Observe → Understand → Predict → Act**

AEROVEDA is an environmental intelligence platform designed to move beyond city-level air-quality monitoring by combining multiple environmental signals to identify **where pollution is emerging, understand the conditions surrounding it, anticipate how it may evolve, and support faster response.**

## 🌍 The Problem

Air pollution is often represented through city-level AQI values.

However, pollution does not always behave uniformly across an entire city.

A sudden emission event near an industrial zone, traffic corridor, construction site, or poorly ventilated urban area can create a **hyper-local pollution event** that may not be adequately represented by a limited number of monitoring stations.

This creates an important gap:

> **We can measure pollution, but detecting exactly where it is emerging, understanding why it is happening, and anticipating what happens next requires multiple sources of intelligence.**

AEROVEDA is designed around this problem.
